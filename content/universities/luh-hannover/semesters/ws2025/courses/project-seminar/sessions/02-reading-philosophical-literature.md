# Session 2: Reading Philosophical Literature

## Aim

Reading and understanding philosophical literature is a learnable,
cultivable skill, not something trivial. Covers structuring your reading,
note-taking, identifying theses, summarizing, and asking relevant
questions. Introduces the "They Say / I Say" scheme for structuring
argument.

## Pre-reading

- Rudner, Richard (1953), "The Scientist qua Scientist Makes Value
  Judgments."
