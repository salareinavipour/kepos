# Models and Measurement (Blockseminar)

## Description

An introduction to the philosophy (and history) of measurement, built
around Hasok Chang's *Inventing Temperature: Measurement and Scientific
Progress* (2004). Central questions: How have measurement standards
emerged and evolved? What's the relationship between modeling and
measurement? What can and can't be measured? What warrants a claim to an
accurate measurement? How can one tell whether an instrument measures what
it's meant to measure?

## Required reading

Hasok Chang (2004), *Inventing Temperature: Measurement and Scientific
Progress*. Oxford University Press — available via campus license; a
physical copy is also in the philosophy department library.
