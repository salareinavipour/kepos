# Session 10: Towards a direct role for values

## Required reading

- Tim Lewens (2025), “Towards a Direct Role for Values in the Heart of Science,” *Philosophy of Science* 92(4): 850–867.

## Focus

Part 4, a new orthodoxy on the horizon? Key questions: should values be brought back into the “heart of science”? Can values play a direct role in accepting hypotheses without amounting to wishful thinking? Can normative claims count as evidence for descriptive hypotheses?
