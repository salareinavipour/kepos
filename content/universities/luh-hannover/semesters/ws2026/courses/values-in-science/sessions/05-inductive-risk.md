# Session 5: Inductive risk

## Required reading

- Richard Rudner (1953), “The Scientist qua Scientist Makes Value Judgments,” *Philosophy of Science* 20(1): 1–6.
- Richard C. Jeffrey (1956), “Valuation and Acceptance of Scientific Hypotheses,” *Philosophy of Science* 23(3): 237–246.

## Focus

Part 2, value-laden science: the current orthodoxy. Key questions: why might evidence alone fail to determine which hypothesis to accept? Why must scientists decide how much evidence is enough, and why does that involve values? What distinguishes these two arguments?
