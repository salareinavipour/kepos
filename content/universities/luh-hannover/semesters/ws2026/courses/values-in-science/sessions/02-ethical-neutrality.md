# Session 2: Ethical neutrality

## Required reading

- Max Weber (1949), “The Meaning of ‘Ethical Neutrality’ in Sociology and Economics,” in *Max Weber on the Methodology of the Social Sciences*, Shils and Finch (eds.), Free Press, pp. 1–47.

## Focus

Part 1, the value-free ideal. Key questions: what is the value-free ideal, and what claims make it up? Why did it come to be seen as common sense? Is it a description of how science works or a norm for how it ought to work?
