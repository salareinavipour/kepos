# Session 8: Democratic legitimacy ideal

## Required reading

- Greg Lusk (2021), “Does Democracy Require Value-Neutral Science? Analyzing the Legitimacy of Scientific Information in the Political Sphere,” *Studies in History and Philosophy of Science Part A* 90: 102–110.

## Focus

Part 3, which values, and whose? Key questions: if values are unavoidable, which influences are legitimate? Can values in science be “policed,” and how? Whose values should guide scientific judgements?
