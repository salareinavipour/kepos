# Values in Science (Seminar)

## Description

How do ethical, social, and political values shape scientific research, and
which of those influences are legitimate? The seminar moves from the
value-free ideal, through the arguments from underdetermination and inductive
risk and the distinction between direct and indirect roles for values, to
recent debates on demarcating legitimate from illegitimate value influence,
democratic legitimacy, partisan science, and proposals for a direct, even
evidential, role for values.

## Learning objectives

- Reconstruct the value-free ideal, separate its component claims, and explain
  why it came to be treated as common sense.
- Characterize the arguments from underdetermination and from inductive risk
  and say how they differ.
- Distinguish different roles values can play in science.
- Assess proposals for telling legitimate from illegitimate value influences.
- Evaluate recent proposals that values may play a direct or evidential role.
- Apply these views to a case study from a scientific discipline.
