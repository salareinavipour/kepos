# The Ethics of Artificial Intelligence (Seminar)

## Description

Ethical questions about AI, ordered roughly by time horizon. Near term: bias
in algorithmic decision-making, privacy and surveillance, automation and
unemployment. Mid term: governing increasingly autonomous systems (self-driving
cars, autonomous weapons) and assigning responsibility for what they do. Long
term: machine consciousness, moral status, and whether artificial agents could
be owed moral consideration comparable to humans.

These are examined through utilitarian, deontological, virtue-ethical, and
justice-based approaches, alongside principles such as harm prevention,
fairness and non-discrimination, respect for autonomy, privacy,
accountability, and moral status.

## Required text

S. Matthew Liao (ed.) (2020), *Ethics of Artificial Intelligence*, Oxford
University Press Additional readings are distributed separately.
