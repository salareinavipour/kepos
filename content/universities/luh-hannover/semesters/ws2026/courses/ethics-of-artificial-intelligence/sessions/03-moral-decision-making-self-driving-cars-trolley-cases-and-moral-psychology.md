# Session 3: Moral decision-making: self-driving cars, trolley cases, and moral psychology

## Required reading

- F. M. Kamm, “The Use and Abuse of the Trolley Problem: Self-Driving Cars, Medical Treatments, and the Distribution of Harm,” *The Ethics of Artificial Intelligence*, pp. 79–108.
- Bonnefon, Shariff, and Rahwan, “The Moral Psychology of AI and the Ethical Opt-Out Problem,” *The Ethics of Artificial Intelligence*, pp. 109–126.
