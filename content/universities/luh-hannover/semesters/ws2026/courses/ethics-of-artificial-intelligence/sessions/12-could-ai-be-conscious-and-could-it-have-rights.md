# Session 12: Could AI be conscious, and could it have rights?

## Required reading

- Susan Schneider, “How to Catch an AI Zombie: Testing for Consciousness in Machines,” *The Ethics of Artificial Intelligence*, pp. 439–458.
- S. Matthew Liao, “The Moral Status and Rights of Artificial Intelligence,” *The Ethics of Artificial Intelligence*, pp. 480–504.

## Optional reading

- Eric Schwitzgebel and Mara Garza, “Designing AI with Rights, Consciousness, Self-Respect, and Freedom,” *The Ethics of Artificial Intelligence*, pp. 459–479.
