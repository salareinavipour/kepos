# Explaining Consciousness (Seminar)

## Description

What explains consciousness, and can philosophy or science explain it at all?
The seminar compares attempts to explain mind, self, and subjective experience
using philosophy, psychology, cognitive science, neuroscience, AI, literature,
and science fiction. Topics: personal identity, self-consciousness, artificial
intelligence, the mind–body relation, and whether consciousness could emerge
from computation or complex information processing. The guiding question:
*what would count as an explanation of consciousness?*

## Required text

Douglas R. Hofstadter and Daniel C. Dennett (eds.), *The Mind’s I: Fantasies
and Reflections on Self and Soul* (Basic Books, 1981/2000); the 1981 edition
is also acceptable. Additional readings are distributed separately.
