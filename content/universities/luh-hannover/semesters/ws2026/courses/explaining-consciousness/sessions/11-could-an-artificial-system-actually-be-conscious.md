# Session 11: Could an artificial system actually be conscious?

## Required reading

- Stanisław Lem, “The Seventh Sally, or How Trurl’s Own Perfection Led to No Good.” In Hofstadter and Dennett, eds., *The Mind’s I* (course textbook).
- David J. Chalmers, “Could a Large Language Model Be Conscious?” arXiv (2023).

## Optional reading

- Patrick Butlin et al., “Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence: Insights from the Science of Consciousness,” arXiv (2023).
- Blackmore and Troscianko, *Consciousness: An Introduction*, 4th ed., ch. 12, “The Evolution of Machines.”
