# Session 10: Does consciousness cause our actions?

## Required reading

- Raymond Smullyan, “Is God a Taoist?” In Hofstadter and Dennett, eds., *The Mind’s I* (course textbook).
- Lieke Joske Franci Asma, “The Relationship Between Free Will and Consciousness,” *Phenomenology and the Cognitive Sciences* 23 (2024): 823–839.

## Optional reading

- Selections from Daniel M. Wegner, *The Illusion of Conscious Will* (MIT Press, 2002).
- Blackmore and Troscianko, *Consciousness: An Introduction*, 4th ed., ch. 9, “Agency and Free Will.”
