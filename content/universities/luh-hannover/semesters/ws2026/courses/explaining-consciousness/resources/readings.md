# Reading list — Explaining Consciousness

## Course textbook

- Hofstadter, Douglas R., and Daniel C. Dennett (eds.) (1981/2000), *The
  Mind’s I: Fantasies and Reflections on Self and Soul*, Basic Books.
  Pieces used: Nagel, Morowitz, Borges, Harding, Hofstadter (“Prelude …
  Ant Fugue”, “A Conversation with Einstein’s Brain”), Zuboff, Turing,
  Searle, Sanford, Smullyan, Lem.

## Additional readings (distributed separately)

- Frankish (2016), “Illusionism as a Theory of Consciousness,” *JCS* 23(11–12).
- Carruthers (2005), “Reductive Explanation and the ‘Explanatory Gap’.”
- Thompson and Zahavi (2007), *The Phenomenological Mind* (selections).
- Albantakis et al. (2023), “Integrated Information Theory (IIT) 4.0,” *PLOS Comput. Biol.* 19(10).
- Seth and Bayne (2022), “Theories of Consciousness,” *Nat. Rev. Neurosci.* 23.
- Harnad (2003), “Can a Machine Be Conscious? How?” *JCS* 10(4–5).
- Wiese (2024), “Artificial Consciousness: A Perspective from the Free Energy Principle,” *Phil. Studies* 181.
- Parfit (1987), “Divided Minds and the Nature of Persons,” in *Mindwaves*.
- Asma (2024), “The Relationship Between Free Will and Consciousness,” *Phenom. Cogn. Sci.* 23.
- Chalmers (2023), “Could a Large Language Model Be Conscious?” arXiv.
- Wagner-Altendorf (2024), “Progress in Understanding Consciousness?” *Acta Analytica* 39.

## Optional readings

- Blackmore and Troscianko (2024), *Consciousness: An Introduction*, 4th ed.,
  Routledge: chs. 2, 9, 11, 12, 16.
- Mehta (2013), “How to Explain the Explanatory Gap,” *Dialectica* 67(2).
- Dennett (2007), “Heterophenomenology Reconsidered,” *Phenom. Cogn. Sci.* 6.
- Mashour et al. (2020), “Conscious Processing and the Global Neuronal Workspace Hypothesis,” *Neuron* 105(5).
- Noë (2009), *Out of Our Heads* (selections).
- Sloman and Chrisley (2003), “Virtual Machines and Consciousness,” *JCS* 10(4–5).
- Olson (2020), “Against Person Essentialism,” *Mind* 129(515).
- Wegner (2002), *The Illusion of Conscious Will* (selections).
- Butlin et al. (2023), “Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence,” arXiv.
