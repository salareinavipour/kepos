# Morality and Justice (Seminar)

Also listed for BA/M.Ed. Philosophie (Aufbaumodul Praktische Philosophie /
Aufbaumodul Master) and M.Ed. Werte und Normen (Vertiefungsmodul
Praktische Philosophie).

## Description

A survey of classic and contemporary answers to core questions in ethics:
what makes a good life, how we should act toward others, what makes an
action right or wrong, and when we're obliged to help. Moves from ancient
sources (Epicurus, Plato, Aristotle) through early modern and modern moral
theory (Hobbes, Mill, Kant) to contemporary applied ethics (Peter Singer,
Judith Jarvis Thomson), using challenging cases and thought experiments
throughout.

## Required text

Russ Shafer-Landau (ed.) (2023), *The Ethical Life: Fundamental Readings
in Ethics and Moral Problems*, 6th ed. Oxford University Press. Additional
readings distributed via the course platform.
